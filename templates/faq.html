Everything you need to know about using YT Downloader.

General
Yes, completely free. It's self-hosted - you run it on your own machine at no cost, forever.
Downloading for personal, offline use is a legal grey area that varies by country. Only download content you own or have permission to use. Never redistribute copyrighted material.
No. Everything runs locally on your machine. No analytics, no cookies, no data sent to any external server.
Downloads
Video + audio (merged MP4), video only, or audio only as MP3. Resolutions from 480p up to 4K depending on what YouTube provides.
Files are saved to the downloads/ folder in the project directory. You can change this in app.py by editing the DOWNLOAD_FOLDER variable.
YouTube Shorts work exactly like regular videos. For playlists, only the first video is downloaded by default. You can change this in app.py.
Troubleshooting
Common causes: the video is age-restricted or private, yt-dlp needs an update (pip install -U yt-dlp), ffmpeg is not installed or not in your PATH, or the URL is malformed.
Speed depends on your internet connection and YouTube's servers. Selecting a lower resolution will significantly reduce download time. The tool itself adds no overhead.
