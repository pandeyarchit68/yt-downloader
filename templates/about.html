About this project

Built for simplicity

YT Downloader is a lightweight, self-hosted tool that lets you save YouTube videos and audio, privately, without ads, and without compromise.

The mission

Most online downloaders are cluttered with ads, require sign-ups, or quietly harvest your data. This project exists as an alternative - a clean, honest tool that does exactly one thing well.

Everything runs on your own machine. No data is sent anywhere. No accounts. No subscriptions.

How it works
1
Paste your URL
Drop any YouTube link into the input - videos, Shorts, or playlists.
2
Choose your format
Select video + audio, video only, or MP3. Pick a resolution up to 4K.
3
Download instantly
yt-dlp fetches the stream, ffmpeg processes it, and the file is delivered to you.
