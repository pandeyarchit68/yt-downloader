Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

TL;DR

We collect nothing. Everything runs on your machine. No tracking, no cookies, no data sent anywhere.

No data collection

YT Downloader runs entirely on your local machine. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage statistics, or download history to any external server.

Local processing only

All downloads are processed locally using yt-dlp and ffmpeg. The only network requests made are directly to YouTube's servers to fetch the video you requested.

Thumbnail and metadata

When you paste a YouTube URL, the app may fetch thumbnail images and video metadata directly from YouTube's public APIs. No data about this request is logged or stored by us.

No cookies or tracking

This application does not use cookies, analytics, session tracking, or any third-party tracking scripts of any kind.

Third-party services

The only third-party service involved is YouTube itself. YouTube's own Privacy Policy applies to those requests.
