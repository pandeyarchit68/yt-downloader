Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

No Data Collection

YT Downloader runs entirely on your local machine. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage statistics, or download history to any external server.

Local Processing Only

All downloads are processed locally using yt-dlp and ffmpeg. The only network requests made are directly to YouTube's servers to fetch the video you requested.

Thumbnail Previews

When you paste a YouTube URL, the app fetches a thumbnail image and video metadata directly from YouTube's public APIs (img.youtube.com and youtube.com/oembed). No data about this request is logged or stored by us.

No Cookies or Tracking

This application does not use cookies, analytics, or any third-party tracking scripts.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service involved is YouTube itself, when fetching video content. YouTube's own Privacy Policy applies to those requests.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us.
